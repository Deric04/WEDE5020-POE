About Bakes by MK

Our Story

Bakes by MK is a small heart filled bakery business operating between Groblersdal, Limpopo and Centurion, Gauteng.

The business started with a brother and sister who shared a love for baking muffins for their family almost every weekend.

The kitchen became a place of solace where creativity could be expressed through baking. The idea behind Bakes by MK began as an experiment to discover whether people would be willing to try and support their baking skills.

Over time, the experiment developed into a growing business. Now in its second year of operation, Bakes by MK has gained significant traction on TikTok, attracting followers as well as customers and orders.

About Our Bakery

Bakes by MK provides freshly baked products for everyday enjoyment and special occasions.

We focus on creating attractive, fresh and high-quality baked products while providing customers with a convenient enquiry process.

Our products include cakes, cupcakes, brownies, cookies, pastries and celebration cakes. We also offer customised cake designs for customers with specific requirements.

Our Mission

The mission of Bakes by MK is to provide freshly prepared high-quality baked goods that bring people together and make everyday moments and special celebrations more memorable.

Our Vision

The vision of Bakes by MK is to become a trusted local bakery known for quality products, creative designs, reliable service and customer satisfaction.
