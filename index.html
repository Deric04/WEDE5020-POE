Welcome to Bakes by MK

Bakes by MK is a small bakery business built around the love of baking and creating delicious products for family, friends and customers.

What started as a brother and sister experimenting with muffins for the family almost every weekend has grown into a growing bakery business with customers and followers on social media.

We create freshly baked products for everyday enjoyment, celebrations, parties and special occasions.
